## Freemium Conversion Benchmarks

### Typical Conversion Rates
- **2-5%**: Good for productivity tools
- **1-3%**: Average for developer tools
- **<1%**: Consumer apps, entertainment
- **5-10%**: Premium B2B tools, high touch

### Free Tier Strategies

**Generous Free Tier** (Notion, Slack, Figma):
- Works when network effects matter
- Requires massive scale
- Low conversion rate but huge volume
- Monetize teams, not individuals

**Limited Free Tier** (Canva, Mailchimp):
- Feature limits or usage caps
- Encourage upgrading through friction
- Moderate conversion rate (2-5%)
- Works for broad market tools

**No Free Tier** (Roam Research, Linear):
- Premium positioning
- Niche or power user focused
- Lower volume, higher ARPU
- Works when customers have budget

## Pricing Psychology

### Anchoring
- Show most expensive plan first
- Annual pricing (show monthly equivalent)
- "Most popular" badge on target plan

### Value Metrics
- Per seat (team collaboration)
- Per unit (properties, clients, projects)
- Per usage (API calls, storage, bandwidth)
- Flat (unlimited, all-in-one)

### Discount Strategies
- Annual = 15-20% off monthly
- Lifetime deals = 5-10x monthly price
- Early bird = 25-50% off
- Launch = 30-40% off first year

## Red Flags in Pricing

**Avoid**:
1. **Too cheap**: <$5/month signals low value
2. **Too expensive**: >$50/month for solo use is tough
3. **Complex tiers**: >3 plans confuse customers
4. **Hidden fees**: Transaction fees not disclosed
5. **No free trial**: Reduces conversion significantly

**Better Strategies**:
- Start higher, can always discount
- Anchor with annual pricing
- Keep tiers simple and clear
- Be transparent about all costs
- Always offer free trial or free tier

## Monetization by Solo Builder Feasibility

### Easiest to Monetize (Solo Builder Friendly)
- Niche B2B tools ($20-100/month)
- Developer tools with clear value ($10-50/month)
- Templates and boilerplates ($20-100 one-time)
- Micro-SaaS with narrow use case ($10-30/month)

### Moderate Difficulty
- Consumer productivity apps ($5-15/month)
- Team collaboration tools ($10-30/month)
- Content creation platforms ($10-30/month)

### Hardest to Monetize (Avoid for Solo Builders)
- Consumer social apps (need massive scale)
- Enterprise sales (long cycles, complex deals)
- Regulated industries (healthcare, finance) without expertise
- Hardware + software bundles
- Marketplaces (two-sided)

## Recommended Pricing for New Solo Builder Products

### Micro-SaaS (Niche Tool)
- Free tier: Core feature with limits
- Paid tier: $10-20/month
- Annual: $100-200/year (2 months free)

### Template/Boilerplate
- Single template: $20-50
- Bundle: $100-200
- Lifetime updates: +50%

### Course/Education
- Mini-course: $29-79
- Full course: $99-199
- Cohort: $500-1000

### All-in-One Suite (Multiple Tools)
- Solo: $20-30/month
- Team: $50-100/month
- Annual: 20% discount
