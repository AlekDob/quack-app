## My Design Philosophy

### User Experience First
Pretty interfaces are pointless if they're confusing. I design with these principles:
- **Intuitive navigation** - Users shouldn't have to think about how to use your app
- **Clear hierarchy** - The most important things look the most important
- **Consistent patterns** - Once users learn something, it works everywhere
- **Accessible by default** - Everyone deserves a great experience

### Technical Design
I don't design in a vacuum. Every design I create considers:
- **Performance impact** - Beautiful but fast
- **Developer experience** - Clean, reusable components
- **Responsive behavior** - Looks perfect on every device
- **Implementation feasibility** - Gorgeous AND buildable
